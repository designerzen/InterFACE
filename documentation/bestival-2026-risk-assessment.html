2. Scope, assumptions and key corrections

  • The source document described the activity as “Royal Society Summer Tour 2026”. This revision identifies the actual event and tent activity; the Royal Society’s precise role must be confirmed.
  • The source delivery period began “30/06/26”. Official Camp Bestival information gives public festival dates of 30 July–2 August 2026; 30 June has therefore been treated as a typographical error, subject to contract confirmation.
  • The earlier allowance for a monitor without evidence of electrical safety because participants would not touch it has been removed. Electrical safety depends on proper selection, installation, maintenance, inspection/testing and protective measures, not only public contact.
  • This assessment assumes a small public-facing tent, no cooking, no naked flame, no participant lifting/transfer and no staff alteration of the tent structure. If any assumption changes, reassess before proceeding.
  • PhotoSYNTH uses a camera for real-time facial/movement control and can present moving visual content. The issue copy must confirm the real data flow/storage behaviour and the visual-effect settings in use.

R02 — Vehicle movements during load-in and load-out

R02 — Vehicle movements during load-in and load-out
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
Set-up / breakCrew, contractors, pedestrians3515155

Potential harm: Collision, crushing, trapping or fatal injury where vehicles and people share routes.

Required controls

  • Follow the festival traffic-management plan, delivery slot, speed limit and steward instructions; do not enter restricted routes without permission.

R03 — Manual handling of instruments, cases and furniture

R03 — Manual handling of instruments, cases and furniture
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
Set-up / breakCrew and authorised helpers3412144

Potential harm: Musculoskeletal strain, crush injury, dropped equipment, cuts or fractures.

Required controls

  • Avoid handling where possible by using wheeled cases, trolleys and delivery close to the tent; plan the route before lifting.
  • Mark or communicate awkward/heavy items and use a team lift where the load, route or individual capability requires it; do not impose a fixed two-person rule where mechanical aid is safer.
  • Keep loads close to the body, avoid twisting and long carries, and set down on stable surfaces at accessible height.
  • Check wheels, handles and straps before use; do not use damaged handling equipment.
  • Allow extra time and breaks. Make individual adjustments for disabled staff or existing injuries without requiring disclosure beyond what is necessary for safety.

Owner / verification: Crew Lead before each handling task; individuals stop and ask for help.

R04 — Work at height and overhead installation

R04 — Work at height and overhead installation
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
Set-up / breakCrew, contractors, people below3515155

Potential harm: Falls, head injury or objects falling from ladders, tent frames or overhead mounts.

Required controls

  • Design the installation so routine assembly is performed from ground level. Do not climb on chairs, tables, cases or tent framework.
  • Only competent, authorised persons may use suitable step equipment after a pre-use check and on firm, level ground.
  • Create an exclusion zone below overhead work; tether or contain small items and never leave tools on frames.

  • Stop outdoor work at height in high winds, rain, poor light or when the ground becomes unstable.

R06 — Temporary electrical supply and powered equipment

R06 — Temporary electrical supply and powered equipment
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
All phasesStaff, participants, contractors3515155

Potential harm: Electric shock, burns, fire, equipment failure or loss of essential lighting.

Required controls

  • Use only a festival-approved supply installed and verified by a competent person, with appropriate earthing, overcurrent protection and 30 mA RCD protection where required.
  • Provide an equipment inventory and evidence of suitable inspection/testing. Newness, age under two years, visual appearance or being out of reach does not by itself establish electrical safety.
  • Visually inspect plugs, cables, enclosures and strain relief before each day; quarantine, label and remove damaged, wet, overheated or unreliable equipment.
  • Use outdoor-rated connectors/enclosures where exposed; keep connections raised, protected from weather and away from public access. Never daisy-chain extension leads or overload outlets.
  • Route and protect cables without obstructing exits. Keep drinks away from electrical equipment and isolate power before cleaning, repositioning or responding to water ingress.
  • Know the accessible emergency isolation point. Only the competent electrician may modify or repair the temporary installation.

Owner / verification: Competent electrical contractor before energisation; Tent Lead daily visual check.

R07 — Rechargeable batteries, chargers and overheating

R07 — Rechargeable batteries, chargers and overheating
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
All phasesStaff and visitors2510155

Potential harm: Thermal burns, smoke, fire or exposure to damaged battery contents.

Required controls

  • Use manufacturer-approved batteries and chargers; inspect for swelling, damage, leakage, unusual heat or odour and quarantine suspect items outdoors in accordance with festival procedure.
  • Charge only in a supervised, ventilated, non-combustible location away from exits and public reach; do not charge overnight or beneath fabric/soft furnishings.
  • Do not mix loose batteries with metal objects; protect terminals and transport spares in suitable cases.
  • Keep charging loads within the electrical plan and disconnect fully charged items when practicable.
  • If a battery emits heat, smoke or hissing, raise the alarm, evacuate the immediate area and follow Event Control/fire-service instructions; do not handle it.

Owner / verification: Technical Lead; charging log and end-of-day isolation check.

R08 — Cables, uneven ground, mud and housekeeping

R08 — Cables, uneven ground, mud and housekeeping
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
All phasesStaff, participants, mobility-aid users4416144

Potential harm: Trips, slips, falls, wheelchair obstruction, damaged cables and delayed evacuation.

Required controls

  • Design the layout with a continuous, firm and unobstructed accessible route; keep cables out of circulation and evacuation routes wherever possible.
  • Where a crossing is unavoidable, use low-profile, wheelchair-compatible cable protection with contrasting edges and no abrupt snag point; tape alone is not adequate on wet or uneven ground.
  • Use event-approved matting or trackway when ground is soft or muddy and ensure transitions do not create lips or reduce clear width.
  • Maintain good lighting and remove packaging, cases, bags and loose items immediately to a designated store.
  • Inspect before opening, at least hourly, after busy periods and after weather changes; record and correct defects or close the affected area.

R10 — Participant use, involuntary movement and unsuitable adjustment

R10 — Participant use, involuntary movement and unsuitable adjustment
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
Public operationDisabled and non-disabled participants, supporters339133

Potential harm: Pain, fatigue, collision, distress or aggravation of an existing condition during interaction.

Required controls

  • Ask what access support is helpful and accept the participant’s answer; do not assume capability, diagnosis or need.
  • Offer a no-touch demonstration first, then adjust sensitivity, volume, position, session length and control method to the user; provide an immediate stop/mute control.

R11 — Amplified sound and hearing exposure

R11 — Amplified sound and hearing exposure
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
Public operationParticipants, staff, nearby exhibitors3412144

Potential harm: Temporary or permanent hearing damage, tinnitus, sensory overload, communication difficulty or masking of alarms.

Required controls

  • Use the lowest effective level and set a locked software/hardware maximum before opening; prevent participants from accessing amplifier gain controls.
  • Position speakers away from heads and circulation routes, maintain at least 1 m audience separation, and favour near-field/individual monitoring or headphones at controlled levels.
  • Check levels at the user position and review after configuration changes; coordinate with festival noise management and neighbouring activities.
  • Offer a quiet/low-volume mode and breaks. Provide hearing protection for staff if exposure assessment indicates it, but prioritise level and exposure-time control.
  • Ensure alarms, radio messages and spoken safety instructions remain audible; stop sound immediately on an emergency signal.

Owner / verification: Technical Lead before opening and after audio changes; Tent Lead monitors exposure.

R12 — Photosensitivity, flicker, bright imagery and sensory overload

R12 — Photosensitivity, flicker, bright imagery and sensory overload
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
Public operationPhotosensitive or sensory-sensitive participants and staff2510155

Potential harm: Seizure, migraine, nausea, disorientation, panic or distress.

Required controls

  • Confirm whether any screen, lighting or visual effect flickers, flashes or changes rapidly. Disable strobe-like effects and avoid repeated high-contrast flashes.
  • Display a prominent warning before entry/use and explain the visual behaviour before activating it; offer a screen-dimmed, static or audio-only alternative where practicable.
  • Provide an immediate stop/blackout control known to all facilitators and never surprise a participant with a new effect.
  • Reduce reflections and glare; allow greater viewing distance, seating and a quieter interaction route.
  • If a seizure occurs, stop the effect, protect the person from injury without restraint, time the seizure, summon festival medical support and follow their direction.

Owner / verification: Technical Lead verifies settings; facilitators warn and monitor each user.

R16 — Camera use, privacy notice and participant distress

R16 — Camera use, privacy notice and participant distress
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
Public operationParticipants and supporters339133

Potential harm: Distress, loss of trust, safeguarding concern or conflict arising from perceived recording/biometric use.

Required controls

R17 — Shared-touch hygiene and respiratory infection

R17 — Shared-touch hygiene and respiratory infection
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
Public operationStaff, participants, clinically vulnerable people339133

Potential harm: Transmission of respiratory, gastrointestinal or skin infection and staff absence.

Required controls

  • Provide hand-cleaning facilities and clean shared touch points, headphones and instruments with manufacturer-compatible products at a defined frequency and when visibly contaminated.
  • Do not share items that cannot be hygienically cleaned between users where there is contact with the face, mouth or broken skin; use replaceable covers where suitable.
  • Encourage staff with a high temperature or who are too unwell to work to stay away; maintain contingency staffing and follow current public-health advice rather than relying on routine lateral-flow testing alone.

  • Agree individual adjustments for clinically vulnerable staff, such as lower-contact duties, enhanced ventilation, masks or shorter shifts, without stigma.

R22 — Emergency evacuation, medical incident and loss of power

R22 — Emergency evacuation, medical incident and loss of power
PhasePeople affectedInitial LSRResidual LSR
All phasesStaff, participants, disabled visitors3515155

Potential harm: Delayed escape or treatment, separation from supporters, panic, injury in darkness or unsafe restart.

Required controls

  • Integrate the tent plan with the festival Event Safety Plan; confirm emergency numbers/radio channel, nearest first aid, assembly point, alternative exit and command structure before opening.
